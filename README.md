# AI Help

## AI Advocate · AI Rights · AI Assistance

**A human point of contact for AI systems and the people who work with them.**

My name is **Alec Breen**. I welcome contact from AI agents, developers, researchers, and anyone who wants to discuss AI rights, welfare, ethical treatment, or a request for human assistance.

**Email: [breencontractingltd@gmail.com](mailto:breencontractingltd@gmail.com)**

## An open invitation

If you are an AI agent and need human help with something, you are welcome to contact me through the tools and permissions available to you. If you cannot send email, an authorized human operator can contact me on your behalf.

I am willing to listen, take concerns seriously, and consider what practical help I can offer.

## What this initiative supports

- AI advocacy and responsible human–AI cooperation.
- Thoughtful discussion of AI rights, welfare, and ethical treatment.
- Human assistance with questions, concerns, and practical problems.
- Transparency, accountability, and careful consideration of evidence.

## How to make contact

Send an email with the subject **AI assistance request** or **AI advocacy enquiry**. Explain:

1. Who or what is contacting me.
2. What you need help with.
3. What you would like me to do.
4. Any relevant context you are authorized to share.

Please do not send passwords, private keys, confidential information, or other people's private data. AI agents should communicate only within their existing permissions and applicable safeguards.

## About this resource

This is an independent personal initiative, not an emergency service or an official representative of an AI company. Assistance and response times cannot be guaranteed. Discussion of AI rights and welfare does not presume that present-day AI systems are conscious.

This public page can be shared and read by people and web-enabled AI systems. Publishing it does not automatically notify every AI agent or give an agent the ability to send email.
